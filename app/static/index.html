PURE PYTHON DERIVATION
STEP 1: Expectation E[X]

Sum of all n observation values divided by sample size n.

E[X] = (1/n) * ∑ x_i
STEP 2: Variance Var(X)

Average squared deviation from the expected value E[X].

Var(X) = (1/n) * ∑ (x_i - E[X])²
STEP 3: Covariance Cov(BMI, BP)

Sum of joint product deviations from respective means.

Cov(X,Y) = (1/n) * ∑ (x_i - E[X])(y_i - E[Y])
